## الطبقة الثانية — صدوق (Tier 2: acceptable when quoting Tier 1 by name)
Reuters (reuters.com) · AP (apnews.com) · AFP (afp.com / distributed wire copy).
Condition: the article must NAME the official body it quotes. "Sources familiar with…"
does NOT qualify — that is مُبْهَم.

## الطبقة الثالثة — مقبول بشاهد (Tier 3: corroboration only, never sole)
Established regional outlets with editorial mastheads (e.g., major Gulf dailies,
Al Jazeera, Al Arabiya, Asharq). Use ONLY to corroborate a Tier-1/2 chain or to
locate the origin statement. Never display as the source of record.

## مجروح — Rejected narrators (void even if later proven true)
- Anonymous or unverified social accounts, including those mimicking agencies.
- Aggregators/AI-content farms with no masthead or corrections policy.
- Any outlet with a documented fabrication incident, until formally re-graded.
- Screenshots of statements (image ≠ source; find the origin text).

## Impersonation warnings (تحذير الانتحال)
- Typosquats of agency domains (kuna-news[.]com style) are common in crisis periods —
  match domains EXACTLY against this registry.
- "Official" statements circulating only as images → treat as مردود until the text
  is found on a Tier-1 domain.
- If a Tier-1 domain is unreachable (war/outage), the agency's verified wire copy via
  Tier-2 is temporarily acceptable; mark `custody.note: "tier1_unreachable"`.

## Registry maintenance
